Rope access makes it possible to reach facades, stairwells, chimneys and hard-to-reach points without erecting scaffolding. It is useful when you need a targeted, quick job or the space makes scaffolding impractical.

## When you need it
- Small repairs at height on facades and balcony edges
- Checks for cracks, detachments and infiltration
- Cleaning and treatment of tall walls
- Urgent jobs where scaffolding would be disproportionate

## How we work
1. Checking the area to reach and the way of access
2. Operators trained for work at height
3. Carrying out the job with suitable equipment
4. Photographic documentation of the work done

## Who it is for
Condominiums, private clients and industry professionals. We are the go-to company for anyone who wants to renovate and enhance their property.
