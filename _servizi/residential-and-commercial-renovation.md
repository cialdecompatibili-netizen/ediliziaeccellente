We renovate flats, houses, shops and offices, from a single room to the whole building. We carry out the required building works and coordinate specialist trades, so the client has a single point of contact from start to finish.

## Works included
- Full and partial renovations
- Residential and commercial renovations
- Demolition and reconstruction
- Masonry and partition walls
- Plastering, skim coats and painting
- New floors and wall coverings
- Restoration and damp-proofing of masonry

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works

Technical activities reserved to licensed professionals and specialist works are carried out through specialised professionals.
