A drone can inspect roofs, facades and overhangs without scaffolding or operators at height. Close-up images help to spot critical points and plan the work.

## When you need it
- Roofs that are hard to reach
- Tall facades to check
- Preliminary check before a quote
- Check after bad weather

## How we work
1. Planning the flight and the shots
2. Capturing photos and video of the areas of interest
3. Analysis of the images
4. Photographic report with the issues found

## Who it is for
Condominiums, private clients and industry professionals. We are the go-to company for anyone who wants to renovate and enhance their property.
