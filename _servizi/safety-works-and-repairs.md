When part of a building deteriorates, acting quickly stops the damage from getting worse and reduces risk for residents and passers-by. Safety works remove the immediate danger; the repair restores the element to its function.

## When you need it
- Plaster or concrete coming away from facades, cornices and balcony edges
- Exposed or rusted reinforcement bars
- Cracks, detachments and water infiltration
- Unstable parts on balconies, gutters and overhangs

## How we work
1. Site inspection and assessment of the condition
2. Securing the parts at risk
3. Repair of deteriorated parts with suitable materials
4. Technical report and photographic report at the end of the job

## Who it is for
Condominiums, private clients and industry professionals. We are the go-to company for anyone who wants to renovate and enhance their property.
