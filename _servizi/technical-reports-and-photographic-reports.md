A good technical report with well-organised photos makes the condition of a property clear and makes it easier to decide what to do. It helps with meetings, quotes and discussions with professionals.

## When you need it
- You need a document for a meeting
- A defect or deterioration has to be described
- You want to compare quotes on the same basis
- You need to document the before and after of the works

## How we work
1. Site inspection and photo survey
2. Technical description of the problems found
3. Indication of the recommended works
4. Delivery of the report in a clear, organised form

## Who it is for
Condominiums, private clients and industry professionals. We are the go-to company for anyone who wants to renovate and enhance their property.
