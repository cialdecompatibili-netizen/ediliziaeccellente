We inspect up close the parts of the building that are hard to reach, without scaffolding. We identify detachments, cracks and critical points and document them with photos and a descriptive report.

## Works included
- Technical surveys at height with rope access
- Inspection of facades, balconies and cornices
- Visual check of the condition of building elements
- Detection of detachments, cracks and critical points
- Inspection of roofs, gutters and downpipes
- Checks of hard-to-reach points
- Photographic documentation of the problems found
- Descriptive technical reports on the state of the site

## How we work
1. Site inspection with technical and photographic report
2. Clear quote before starting
3. Works followed at every stage
4. Final documentation of the works

Technical activities reserved to licensed professionals and specialist works are carried out through specialised professionals.
