A leak rarely fixes itself: water gets in, spreads and damages plaster, floors and services. Waterproofing creates a continuous barrier that protects the structure.

## When you need it
- Damp stains and mould on ceilings and walls
- Plaster and paint coming away
- Water seeping in after rain
- Terrace flooring that holds water

## How we work
1. Finding the cause of the leak
2. Preparing the substrate
3. Applying the waterproofing system suited to the surface
4. Checking joints and critical points

## Who it is for
Condominiums, private clients and industry professionals. We are the go-to company for anyone who wants to renovate and enhance their property.
