Autumn is the right time to check the roof: before heavy rain and cold, many problems can be avoided.

## What to check
- Gutters and downpipes: they must be clear of leaves and debris
- Displaced, broken or missing tiles
- Flashings where the roof meets chimneys and walls
- Sealants and joints
- Damp patches in attics and top floors

## Why it matters
A blocked gutter makes water overflow down the facade, while a tile out of place lets water in.

## What to do
1. Clean gutters and downpipes.
2. Have the roof checked, including a survey at height.
3. Repair weak points at once.
4. Plan extraordinary maintenance if needed.

An annual check costs little compared with a repair after a leak.
