The facade protects the building from rain, sun and temperature swings. When it starts to deteriorate, the problem is not only cosmetic.

## The signs
- Cracks and fissures in the plaster
- Plaster that crumbles or sounds hollow
- Detachments, even small ones, of parts of the cladding
- Dark stains, efflorescence and moss
- Balcony edges and cornices with exposed reinforcement

## Why not wait
Cracks let water in and water speeds up decay. Detachments can also become a hazard for people walking below the building.

## How we proceed
1. Site inspection and assessment of the facade.
2. A report with photos to share the situation with the manager and owners.
3. A clear quote with the necessary works.
4. Execution: removal of damaged parts, plaster restoration and painting.

Planned maintenance avoids urgent and much costlier work.
