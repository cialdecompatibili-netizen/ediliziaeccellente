A leak is rarely visible where it starts. Water gets in at one point of the terrace, runs under the flooring and shows up elsewhere, often on the ceiling of the flat below.

## Signs not to ignore
- Damp patches or rings on the ceiling and walls
- Plaster that swells, crumbles or comes away
- Mould and a musty smell in the rooms below the terrace
- Tiles that lift or joints that open

## Why it happens
Over time the membrane loses elasticity, the sealants crack and the slopes no longer carry water away. Frost and heat do the rest.

## What to do
1. Do not just repaint: the stain comes back if the cause remains.
2. Have the terrace checked by a technician to find where the water gets in.
3. Choose the right work: a localised repair or a new waterproofing layer.
4. Restore the parts damaged by water.

Acting early costs less: limited damage today can become a bigger job tomorrow.
