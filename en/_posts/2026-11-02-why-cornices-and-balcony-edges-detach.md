Cornices and balcony edges are among the elements most exposed to the weather. When they deteriorate, pieces of concrete can come away and fall.

## Why they detach
Water gets in through small cracks and reaches the reinforcing bars. The steel rusts, expands and pushes off the concrete covering it.

## The signs
- Rust-coloured stains
- Exposed reinforcement
- Concrete that crumbles or sounds hollow
- Small detachments under balconies

## What to do
1. Report the situation at once and, if needed, secure the area below.
2. Remove the unstable parts.
3. Treat the steel and restore the concrete.
4. Protect the surface to slow down new decay.

## Prevention
Periodic checks and small timely repairs avoid urgent works. Detachments are also a risk to people: better not to wait.
