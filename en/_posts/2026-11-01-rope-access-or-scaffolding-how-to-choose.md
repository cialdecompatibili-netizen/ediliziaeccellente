There are two ways to work at height: scaffolding and rope access. Neither is best in absolute terms, only the more suitable one for the job.

## When rope access makes sense
- Localised work on facades, cornices or gutters
- Surveys and inspections of hard-to-reach points
- Urgent safety works
- Buildings where erecting scaffolding would be long or complicated

## When scaffolding is needed
For large and lengthy works, such as a complete facade renewal, scaffolding is often still the better choice.

## The advantages of ropes
Quick start-up, less obstruction of the street and courtyard and lower costs on targeted works. The work is done by staff trained to operate at height.

## How to decide
The simplest way is a site inspection: we assess the building, the extent of the work and the site conditions, then propose the most suitable method.
