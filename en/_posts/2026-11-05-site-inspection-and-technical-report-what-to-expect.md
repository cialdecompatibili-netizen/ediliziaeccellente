Before talking about costs you need to understand the real state of the property. That is why every job starts with a site inspection.

## How it works
A technician checks the areas concerned by eye: facades, balconies, cornices, roofs and common areas. Where needed, the check is made at height on ropes or by drone, to see hard-to-reach points too.

## What the report contains
- A description of the state of the site
- Critical points found, such as detachments, cracks and leaks
- A photographic report of the problems
- Proposed works

## What it is for
The report is a clear document to share with the manager and present at the meeting. It helps decide with concrete data and compare proposals.

## The next step
Based on the report we prepare a clear quote before starting any work.
